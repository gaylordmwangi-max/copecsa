Collegiate Examinations

COPECSA administers rigorous collegiate examinations to certify pathologists and maintain the highest professional standards across the ECSA region.

After extensive negotiation and consultative efforts by all the ECSA Health Community constituent Colleges, the Medical Boards and Councils and Ministries of Health in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Malawi, and Zambia have given COPECSA the go-ahead to mount and administer college examinations.
