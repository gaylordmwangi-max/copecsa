Partners & Collaborators

Working Together for Quality Pathology

COPECSA collaborates with leading institutions and organisations to advance pathology practice, build capacity, and strengthen healthcare systems across Africa.

Key Partners

Our Collaborators

African Strategies for Advancing Pathology

ASAP is a key partner in capacity building for pathologists across Africa. Together with COPECSA, ASAP has facilitated training and skills development in cancer diagnosis for pathologists from the ECSA region.

The Royal College of Pathologists

RCPath provides internationally recognised examination standards and collaborative frameworks that support COPECSA's mission to promote excellence in pathology across the ECSA region.

East Africa Division of the International Academy of Pathology

EADIAP is a key partner in advancing pathology knowledge and practice in East Africa, supporting COPECSA's training and professional development programmes.

American Society for Clinical Pathology

ASCP brings world-class pathology expertise and certification standards to the ECSA region, strengthening COPECSA's training and examination programmes.

ECSA Health Community

The ECSA Health Community provides regional health governance frameworks and supports COPECSA's mission to strengthen pathology systems across the member states.

British Division of the International Academy of Pathology

BDIAP supports COPECSA through collaborative educational initiatives, examination standards, and professional exchange programmes for pathologists in the ECSA region.

GEMx

The Global Partnership for Educational Exchange in Medicine and the Health Professions. GEMx sponsors a pilot elective south-to-south programme connecting five universities in the ECSA region.

ECFMG

The Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates. ECFMG, through its foundation FAIMER, supports COPECSA's educational exchange and training programmes.

FAIMER

The Foundation for Advancement of International Medical Education and Research. FAIMER supports medical education initiatives and partnerships that benefit COPECSA's training programmes.

Government Partners

Medical Boards & Ministries of Health

COPECSA works closely with Medical Boards, Councils, and Ministries of Health across the ECSA region to ensure our examinations and programmes meet the highest standards.

Kenya Uganda Tanzania Rwanda Malawi Zambia

College of Pathologists of East, Central and Southern Africa. Promoting excellence in the practice of Pathology since 2012.
