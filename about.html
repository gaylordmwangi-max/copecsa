About COPECSA

Excellence in Pathology for Better Health

Learn about our history, mission, values, and the leaders who guide our work across East, Central and Southern Africa.

Our Mission

To develop highly competent and ethical specialist workforce in Pathology able to provide quality laboratory services for diagnosis, prevention and management of disease and also for research to international standards.

Our Vision

To develop and promote leadership and excellence in the safe practice of Pathology through training, accreditation, advocacy and professional development for quality health.

Our Goal

Establishing a Regional and Internationally Recognized College

To establish a regional and internationally recognized College that supports and informs medical and laboratory practice and ensures quality #PathologyinAfrica.

Our Ethos

Traditional Values, Modern Innovation

COPECSA firmly believes in a fusion of traditional values and modern innovation. It upholds the core principles of excellence, professionalism, integrity, accountability, equity, and ethical practice while also embracing creativity and innovation.

Our History

From APECSA to COPECSA

1991
APECSA Established
The Association of Pathologists of East, Central, and Southern Africa (APECSA) was established, laying the groundwork for a regional College of Pathology.
2012
COPECSA Officially Established
COPECSA was officially established with a constitution, bylaws, and the formation of its first Council. Its primary mission is to promote excellence in the practice of pathology.
2019
Capacity Building & Partnerships
19 pathologists from the ECSA region received capacity building in cancer diagnosis through partnerships with ASAP and Aga Khan University Hospital. A manuscript describing COPECSA's establishment was accepted by the African Journal of Laboratory Medicine.
2020
First Collegiate Examination
Medical Boards and Councils in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Malawi, and Zambia approved COPECSA to mount and administer college examinations. The first collegiate exam was scheduled for 2020.
