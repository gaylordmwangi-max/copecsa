About COPECSA

A College That Promotes Excellence in Pathology

COPECSA aims at promoting excellence in the practice of Pathology and is responsible for maintaining standards through training, examinations and professional development for the benefit of patients and the improved understanding and outcome of disease.

Established in 2012, COPECSA brings together pathologists and pathology departments across East, Central and Southern Africa, working to develop a highly competent and ethical specialist workforce in Pathology.

What We Do

Our Key Areas of Work

COPECSA focuses on three core areas to advance pathology practice and ensure quality healthcare across the ECSA region.

01
Training & Education

Developing the Next Generation of Pathologists

Developing a highly competent and ethical specialist workforce in Pathology through structured training programmes, faculty development, and hands-on experience across the ECSA region.

02
Examinations

Rigorous Standards for Professional Certification

Administering rigorous collegiate examinations to certify pathologists and maintain high professional standards. COPECSA examinations are recognized by Medical Boards and Councils across the region.

03
Professional Development

Building Capacity Across the Continent

Providing continuous professional development opportunities, capacity building in cancer diagnosis, and fellowship programmes for practicing pathologists across Africa.

A Message From Our President
