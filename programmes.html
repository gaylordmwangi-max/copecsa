Our Work

Programmes & Activities

COPECA delivers impactful programmes that advance pathology practice, build capacity, and strengthen healthcare systems across East, Central and Southern Africa.

Training & Education

Developing the Next Generation of Pathologists

COPECSA's training programmes are designed to develop a highly competent and ethical specialist workforce in Pathology. Through structured training, faculty development, and hands-on experience, we prepare pathologists to provide quality laboratory services across the ECSA region.

Our reach for training faculty and junior pathologists in 2019 was 28 in total from across the ECSA region, demonstrating our commitment to expanding pathology capacity on the continent.

Through our pilot elective south-to-south programme, sponsored through GEMx, we are creating new opportunities for pathology trainees to gain international experience within the ECSA region itself.

Capacity Building

Cancer Diagnosis & Skills Development

One of COPECSA's significant achievements was capacity building in cancer diagnosis for 19 pathologists from the ECSA region. This was achieved through partnerships with African Strategies for Advancing Pathology (ASAP) and The Aga Khan University Hospital, Nairobi.

These capacity-building programmes ensure that pathologists across the region have the skills and knowledge needed to provide accurate diagnoses and improve patient outcomes.

Fellowship

Become a COPECSA Fellow

COPECSA confers fellowships on eligible practicing pathologists. Fellowship signifies recognition of expertise and commitment to excellence in pathology practice.

Fellowship is awarded to pathologists who demonstrate significant contributions to the field and meet the rigorous standards set by the College. Fellows are recognized for their expertise, leadership, and dedication to advancing pathology across the ECSA region.

As a COPECSA Fellow, you join a distinguished community of pathologists committed to maintaining the highest standards of laboratory medicine and contributing to improved health outcomes across East, Central and Southern Africa.
